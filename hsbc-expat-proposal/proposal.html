01

In brief

The HSBC Expat app works on one device, with no self-service way to move to a new one. A routine phone change therefore depends on reaching the contact centre — which, in my case, meant more than a week without access to my accounts. HSBC already solves this in the UK; I propose bringing it to HSBC Expat.

02

03

Why it matters

Client trust

Internationally mobile Premier clients change phones, numbers and countries more than most. Being unable to reach one’s own money is the moment a valued relationship is most at risk.

Operating cost

Every device change becomes an avoidable contact-centre call — adding to the very queues customers are struggling to get through.

Conduct & resilience

Customers locked out of their funds for days because of a process gap is exactly the kind of outcome regulators look at closely.

04

Already solved within HSBC

HSBC UK
today

Customers set up a new device in the app using Photo ID and a selfie — no call, no waiting for a code. The capability already exists within the Group; this proposal extends it to HSBC Expat.

Seamless device access · HSBC Expat 03
05

The proposal

Keep the security of the one-device model.
Remove the dependency on a phone call.

01

Sign in

On the new device, with the customer’s existing username and password.

02

Verify

Photo ID and a liveness selfie matched to the KYC record already held, plus a one-time code to the registered phone or email.

03

Retire the old device

The previous device is deregistered automatically, with an instant alert by email and SMS.

04

Protected start

Balances and existing payees available at once; new payees and high-value payments unlock after a 24–72 hour safety window.

+Optional second trusted device, such as a tablet, so one lost phone never means zero access.
+Bookable Premier call-back in the app for the genuine exceptions that still need a person.
06

Outcomes

Minutes, not days

Moving to a new device becomes a short, self-service step.

Fewer calls

Device changes leave the contact-centre queue, freeing it for complex needs.

Stronger security

Old devices revoked instantly; risk-based limits on a new device.
